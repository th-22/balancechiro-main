About Balance Chiropractic

Helping You Move With Balance

Dr. Dat Lu has over 20 years of experience as a chiropractic doctor. Balance Chiropractic Center serves patients in Seattle and the surrounding community with personalized chiropractic care.

Balance Chiropractic Center has helped patients address back, neck, spine, and other musculoskeletal concerns. Dr. Dat Lu specializes in auto injury and workplace injury cases.

Balance Chiropractic Center participates in the following health plans and case types: Regence, Premera, Personal Injury, and Labor & Industry.

Our Mission

Personalized care with a focus on your well-being.

Our mission at Balance Chiropractic is to empower individuals to live their lives to the fullest through personalized chiropractic care. With a dedication to excellence and compassion, we strive to provide care tailored to each patient's unique needs.

By working collaboratively with our patients, we aim to support improved function and help address pain and mobility concerns. We are committed to providing a supportive environment where every individual feels heard, valued, and respected throughout their care.
