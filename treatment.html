How We Help

Care designed around how you move and feel.

In Seattle, chiropractic care is often associated with treating back and neck pain. At Balance Chiropractic Center, our approach focuses on understanding each patient's individual concerns, movement, and goals.

Chiropractic care may be used as part of a broader approach to managing certain musculoskeletal conditions and improving function. We work with our patients to develop care plans that are appropriate for their individual needs.

Our Approach

Supporting your body's ability to move well.

The spine and musculoskeletal system play an important role in everyday movement and physical function. When pain, stiffness, or restricted movement affects your daily activities, chiropractic care may be one component of a personalized treatment plan.

Our goal is to provide thoughtful, individualized care while helping patients better understand their condition and available treatment options.
