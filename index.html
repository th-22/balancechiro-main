About Us

Balance Chiropractic Center is run by Dr. Dat Lu, an experienced chiropractor who has been providing exceptional care to patients in Seattle for over 20 years. Balance Chiropractic Center has helped thousands of patients heal and recover from back, neck, spine, and musculoskeletal injuries. Dr. Dat Lu specializes in auto injury and work place injury cases. Balance Chiropractic Center participates in the following health plans: Regence, Premera, First Choice, Aetna, Cigna, Group Health, Uniform Medical, Medicare, Personal Injury and Labor & Industry.
