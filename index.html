About Us

Balance Chiropractic Center is run by Dr. Dat Lu, an experienced chiropractor who has been providing exceptional care to patients in Seattle for over 20 years. Balance Chiropractic Center has helped thousands of patients heal and recover from back, neck, spine, and musculoskeletal injuries. Dr. Dat Lu specializes in auto injury and work place injury cases. Balance Chiropractic Center participates in the following health plans: Regence, Premera, First Choice, Aetna, Cigna, Group Health, Uniform Medical, Medicare, Personal Injury and Labor & Industry.

Our mission at Balance Chiropractic is to empower individuals to live their lives to the fullest through personalized chiropractic care that promotes holistic well-being. With a dedication to excellence and compassion, we strive to deliver exceptional chiropractic services tailored to each patient's unique needs. By fostering a collaborative partnership with our patients, we aim to restore function, alleviate pain, and enhance overall health, allowing them to thrive and embrace life with vitality and joy. At Balance Chiropractic, we are committed to providing a supportive and nurturing environment where every individual feels heard, valued, and empowered on their journey to optimal wellness.
